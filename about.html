About InvoiceShield

InvoiceShield provides free, straightforward business tools for freelancers, independent contractors and small businesses. Users can create invoices, estimates, payment receipts and purchase orders, calculate totals, add a logo and save professional PDFs without creating an account.

Our approach

We focus on speed, privacy and practical documents. Draft information is stored locally in the user’s browser rather than in an InvoiceShield customer database. Users remain responsible for checking their taxes, contracts, payment terms and legal obligations.
