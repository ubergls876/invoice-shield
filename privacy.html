Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Information processed by the tools

Invoice and document information entered into InvoiceShield is processed in your browser. Draft fields and an uploaded logo may be stored in your browser’s local storage so they remain after a refresh. InvoiceShield does not require a user account and does not operate a customer-document database for these tools.

Hosting data

Our hosting provider may process standard technical data such as IP address, browser information, request time and security logs to deliver and protect the website.

Email

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. Do not send sensitive financial, identity or client data.

Your choices

You can clear locally saved drafts by selecting New Document or clearing this site’s browser data. Blocking local storage may prevent draft saving.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. Questions can be sent to invoiceshieldsupport@gmail.com.
